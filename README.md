# Welcome to DHDK-radar
This is the repo for the collaborative project by [Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge (DHDK)](https://corsi.unibo.it/2cycle/DigitalHumanitiesDigitalKnowledge) 2nd year students (26/27) for a newsletter/bot working as radar for local and online opportunities for DHDK students in Alma Mater Studiorum in Bologna, Italy.

## The aim
The city of Bologna is a great hub for opportunities, events, workshops, all related to many different topics (Digital Humanities in our case...). Sometimes though...it might become difficult to be up to date with everything that is going on. Sometimes information on these events is fragmented, difficult to track or not too much shared to the public.

So our aim is to **collect, structure and share** all these opportunities in one place, both for those happening in the city but also for remote opportunities with online attendance. 
The idea is to automize the distribution of information, direct links to the events, maybe through the use of a Bot (Telegram or independet), or a Newsletter, maybe both??? (We'll see...)

## What will we collect?
We will mainly collect events relevant for our community (DHDK) at first:
- **Local events** (in Bologna or any place that is reachable by bus/train from the city);
- **Online events** (accessible from anywhere);
- **Main topics:** Tech, Digital Humanities, AI, GLAM, Web Development, Arts & Culture, whatever might be interesting for us and for you (open for any suggestions!!!)
